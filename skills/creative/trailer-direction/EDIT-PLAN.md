# Music-led edit plan

A rough treatment needs fewer fields than an editor-ready timeline. Use what serves the request.

- **Purpose:** experience or action to communicate.
- **Sources:** track version, footage pool, references, contract, usage status where known.
- **Structure:** hook, reveal, gameplay proof, escalation, title, call to action when useful; order can vary.
- **Timing:** range, musical cue, shot, text, transition, sound treatment, production dependency.
- **Variants:** crop, length, captions, or no-audio treatment when requested.

| Time or cue | What the viewer sees | Music and sound | Production need |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Verified timestamp or provisional phrase cue | Shot purpose, action, text | Vocal entry, accent, pause, effect, mix change | Available capture, staged shot, graphic, unresolved dependency |

Label provisional timing. A measured timestamp and suggested duration are different kinds of information.

For reference analysis, attach the specific moment and explain the transferable technique. Preserve the target's identity instead of copying a sequence shot for shot.
