# Procedural visual review

Inspect the rendered result, not just source or absence of errors.

- **Identity:** does the silhouette read at intended scale without color or labels alone?
- **Contract:** do contour, proportions, palette, material, light, and composition follow direction?
- **Controls:** does each parameter change its stated property without breaking unrelated identity?
- **Behavior:** inspect poses, deformation extremes, contact, layers, and camera relevant to the request.
- **Export:** check transparency, crop, resolution, orientation, and reproducibility.
- **Runtime:** exercise interaction and check console errors. Measure target performance when required; label estimates.

Use contact sheets for identity across states, several angles for 3D, and neutral lighting when it reveals hidden material problems. Compare the result in its intended setting as well as its study page.

If inspection is unavailable, state what remains unchecked. A successful build establishes executability, not visual fidelity.
